Mukur privacy policy

Updated 21 November 2026

Camera and check-ins

With your permission, Mukur uses the camera for check-in selfies and live exercise cues. It analyses images on your device. Live exercise frames are discarded. Check-in photos are kept in app-private storage while analysis is pending or in progress, including when processing resumes in the background. Mukur deletes its copies after analysis and retries interrupted cleanup. A pending photo can stay until processing finishes or you clear the app’s storage.

Your records

Mukur saves your check-in dates, facial readings, skin entries, practice history and preferences in the app’s local storage. It uses them to show progress and tailor routines and care information. Reminders run on your phone. If you add a widget, your home screen can show completion status. Mukur has no account or server that receives these personal records.

Backups and external services

When you export, Mukur writes an unencrypted JSON file to the destination you choose. It contains your records and can include pending check-in selfies. Anyone with the file can read it and recover those photos. A cloud folder or share recipient can take the file off your device. Care and product links open in your browser under the destination site’s policy. Google Play handles your purchase.

Your choices

You can turn off the camera or notifications in Android Settings. To erase all records and pending photos, open Android Settings, choose Apps, then Mukur, Storage and Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Delete exported backups separately wherever you saved or shared them.
