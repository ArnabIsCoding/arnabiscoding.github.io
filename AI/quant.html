Level 1: The Foundations of Numerical Precision

This first stage establishes the "why" and "what" of quantization. Here you'll learn the core motivations for compressing large models and the fundamental language of numerical representation used in deep learning.

💾

Reduced Memory

Quantization drastically cuts a model's memory footprint by converting 32-bit float weights to smaller types like 8-bit integers, making massive models accessible on consumer hardware.

⚡️

Faster Inference

By reducing the amount of data moved from memory to processor, quantization overcomes the memory-bandwidth bottleneck, leading to significant speedups in model response time.

🔋

Energy Efficiency

Less data movement and faster calculations mean lower power consumption, a crucial benefit for data centers and battery-powered edge devices like phones and drones.
